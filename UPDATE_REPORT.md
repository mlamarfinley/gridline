## 1. Team-color matchup theme
- **Source:** ESPN `color` / `alternateColor` for both teams, from the game summary header.
- **Contrast:** `public/theme.js` adjusts each color's lightness until it has at least 3:1 contrast against the page background (dark or light theme). It falls back to the alternate color when the primary can't be made readable. When the two teams' colors are too similar, one side switches to a neutral fallback. Text placed on a team-color fill is white or near-black, whichever gives at least 4.5:1 (tested).
  - Example: the Raiders' `#000000` becomes their silver `#a5acaf`; KC stays `#e31837`.
- **Where color appears:** header team names and edge bars, sticky team column heads, card top rules, last-five bars, the game-script spectrum (away color on the left, home on the right), the injury and context-table headings, and the grade bars.
- **Background:** a restrained split wash, about 13% of each team's color fading in from its own edge.
- **Unchanged meanings:** model numbers stay lime, book lines stay blue, and injury tags keep their own colors.

## 3. OUTLIER PICK (replaces "standout")
- **Code:** `src/outlier.js` (pure; covered by 9 tests).
- **Candidates:** every tracked stat of every modeled skill player that has an actual posted book line. That includes simulated supporting players, who get their own card if picked. No line means no candidate. Season averages are never used as a substitute line.
- **Ranking:** standardized gap z = (projection − line) / model SD. The SD comes from the simulated 10–90 range, with per-stat floors so discrete stats can't produce inflated z-scores. z is multiplied by a quality factor:

  | Condition | Effect |
  |---|---|
  | Fewer than 2 games this season | Ineligible |
  | Out / Doubtful | Ineligible |
  | Line older than 72h | Ineligible |
  | Model has no role for the stat (range 0–0) but a line is posted | "Role conflict": ineligible on all of that player's stats, and surfaced as a warning |
  | 2–3 games of sample | ×0.85 |
  | Questionable | ×0.7 |
  | Returning from absence | ×0.75 |
  | Recent role change | ×0.9 |

- **Pick rule:** a pick needs an adjusted score of at least 0.45 SD **and** a model P(side) of at least 58%. Otherwise the page shows "No pick" with the reason; no pick is ever forced.
- **Display:** direction (OVER/UNDER), actual line, projection, signed gap and gap %, 10–90 range, z and adjusted score, P(side) with fair odds, line source plus line-updated and retrieval timestamps, evidence (season average vs line, last-five hit count, opportunity), uncertainty flags, the model's reasoning, and a ranked candidate table. It always carries the "experimental · uncalibrated · not a claim the book is wrong" label.

## 4. Estimated OL / DL unit grades
- **Code:** `src/linegrades.js` (7 tests).
- **Components:**
  - **OL run blocking:** designed-run success (QB scrambles excluded) 40%, stuff rate avoided 30%, yards per designed run 20%, 10+ run rate 10%.
  - **OL pass protection:** sack rate avoided.
  - **DL run defense / DL pass rush:** the same metrics as allowed or generated by the defense.
  - **Composites:** OL = 55/45 run/pass; DL = 50/50.
- **Scale:** rates are shrunk toward the baseline (80 runs / 150 dropbacks of prior weight). 50 = baseline unit, ±15 points = one team-level SD, clipped to 0–100, higher always better. Letters: A ≥75, B ≥62, C ≥45, D ≥32, F below. Confidence is low/medium/high by sample size. Missing inputs show "unavailable", never 0.
- **Baseline:**
  - NFL: **measured** from all 48 completed 2026 games before kickoff (32 teams), including the team-level SD. That SD includes sampling noise after 3 games, which makes the grades conservative.
  - College: **configured priors, labeled as such**, because measuring the full FBS field would take hundreds of fetches per page load.
- **Labeling:** no percentile or rank is shown. Grades are labeled team-unit estimates, not individual linemen, and not free of QB, back, scheme, or opponent effects. Sack rate is flagged as QB-influenced.
- **Not included:** strength of schedule beyond shrinkage. The projection model doesn't read the grades. Matchup "edges" (OL vs opposing DL) appear only when the gap is at least 8 points.
- **Web research:** I did not add external rankings or forum sentiment. The credible OL grades (PFF) are paywalled, and fan-forum opinion would be unverifiable. The page shows measured proxies only, so no opinion is presented as a score.

## 5. Why projections sit close to lines (audit)
- **Prop lines never enter the model.** They are read after simulation, purely as the comparison threshold (`src/matchup.js`: `book` → `threshold` / `probOver`).
- **Game total and spread do anchor the model (market-informed mode):**
  - The spread sets the game-script weights, which shift pass/run rates and each player's shares.
  - The implied team points calibrate TD rates and kicker FG volume.
  - The total adjusts pace by at most ±10%.
- **Why the numbers still land near the book:** the model and the book start from the same usage and efficiency history, and the model shrinks small samples toward league means, as books do. Median |gap| on the current slate is 0.24 SD (13%); 85% of lines are within 0.5 SD.
